# Business Performance & Margin Improvement Diagnostic

## Case Company

Toast

## Core Business Question

How can Toast improve operating performance and expand margins while preserving sustainable growth and customer value?

## Management Decision

Management must decide which combination of growth, monetization, cost-efficiency, and operating-productivity initiatives should receive priority over the next 12–24 months.

The analysis should ultimately recommend:

1. where the largest credible performance-improvement opportunities exist
2. which initiatives management should prioritize
3. the expected economic impact of those initiatives
4. the implementation cost, risk, and trade-offs
5. which KPIs management should monitor to determine whether the strategy is working

## Analytical Objective

Build an evidence-based diagnostic that moves through:

```text
Business Performance
        ↓
Revenue & Cost Drivers
        ↓
Margin Diagnosis
        ↓
Improvement Opportunities
        ↓
Scenario Economics
        ↓
Risk & Feasibility
        ↓
Prioritization
        ↓
Executive Recommendation
