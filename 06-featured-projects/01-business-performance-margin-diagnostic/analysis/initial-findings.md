# Initial Findings — Toast Business Performance Diagnostic

## Scope

This note captures preliminary findings from the 2023–2025 revenue, gross profit, gross margin, and segment-mix analysis.

These findings are directional and should not yet be treated as final management recommendations.

## Preliminary Findings

### 1. Consolidated gross margin improved materially

Toast's consolidated gross margin increased from approximately 21.6% in 2023 to 25.9% in 2025, an improvement of approximately 4.31 percentage points.

This indicates meaningful improvement in gross-level operating economics over the period.

### 2. Subscription economics strengthened

Subscription gross margin increased from approximately 66.8% to 71.8%, an improvement of approximately 4.99 percentage points.

Subscription also increased its share of total revenue by approximately 2.28 percentage points.

This suggests Subscription is becoming increasingly important to consolidated margin quality.

### 3. Fintech remains the largest absolute gross-profit growth engine

Fintech contributed approximately 60.6% of the total increase in gross profit between 2023 and 2025.

Its gross margin improved more modestly, by approximately 1.24 percentage points.

This indicates that Fintech remains the primary scale contributor even as its relative share of gross profit declines.

### 4. Hardware & Services remains economically dilutive

Hardware & Services generated negative gross profit throughout the period.

Its gross-profit contribution declined by approximately $39 million between 2023 and 2025 and represented approximately -5.1% of consolidated gross-profit growth over the period.

Its share of total revenue also declined by approximately 1.63 percentage points.

### 5. Margin improvement appears associated with both mix and segment economics

The current evidence is consistent with a combination of:

- stronger Subscription gross margins,
- modest Fintech margin improvement,
- increasing Subscription revenue mix,
- declining Hardware & Services revenue mix,
- and continued strong absolute Fintech gross-profit growth.

The current analysis supports association, not causal attribution.

## Current Management Hypothesis

Toast's gross-margin expansion appears to be driven by a combination of improving segment economics and favorable revenue mix, with Fintech providing scale, Subscription improving margin quality, and Hardware & Services acting as a persistent gross-profit drag.

## Next Analytical Questions

- What operating and commercial factors caused the observed mix shift toward Subscription and away from Hardware & Services?
- What operating-cost drivers sit below gross profit?
- Which margin-improvement opportunities are structurally repeatable versus temporary?
- What initiatives could improve Hardware & Services economics or reduce its drag?
- What implementation costs, risks, and payback periods would apply to each potential initiative?

## Operating Margin Improvement

A review of Toast's 2023–2025 operating performance shows that operating margin improved from -7.43% in 2023 to 4.75% in 2025, a total improvement of 12.17 percentage points.

### 2023–2025 Operating Margin Decomposition

- Gross margin improvement: +4.31 percentage points
- Operating-expense leverage: +7.86 percentage points
- Total operating-margin improvement: +12.17 percentage points

Approximately 35.4% of the operating-margin improvement came from gross-margin expansion, while 64.6% came from operating-expense leverage.

This indicates that Toast’s profitability improvement was driven primarily by scaling operating expenses more efficiently relative to revenue, with gross-margin expansion providing meaningful secondary support.

The largest operating-leverage improvements came from:

- General & Administrative expense
- Research & Development expense

Sales & Marketing also improved as a percentage of revenue, but to a lesser degree.

These findings suggest that Toast’s profitability gains were driven more by operating discipline and scale efficiency than by gross-margin improvement alone.

The analysis is descriptive and does not yet establish the underlying operational causes of each expense-ratio improvement.

### 2023–2025 Margin Change

- Mix effect: +3.06 percentage points
- Within-segment effect: +0.79 percentage points
- Interaction effect: +0.42 percentage points
- Amortization effect: +0.05 percentage points
- Total explained margin change: +4.31 percentage points
- Observed gross-margin change: +4.31 percentage points

Approximately 71% of the observed gross-margin expansion was attributable to favorable revenue mix.

The largest structural drivers were:

- increasing revenue exposure to the higher-margin Subscription business
- decreasing revenue exposure to negative-margin Hardware & Services
- additional support from improving Subscription and Fintech segment margins

This strengthens the current management hypothesis: Toast's margin expansion reflects both mix and operating-economics improvements, but the dominant observed driver from 2023 through 2025 was a more favorable business mix.

The decomposition is descriptive and based on reported segment economics. It identifies mathematical contribution to observed margin change but does not by itself establish the operational causes behind each segment's margin movement.

# Executive Recommendation

Toast materially improved profitability from 2023 to 2025 while continuing to grow revenue at a strong rate. Operating margin improved from -7.43% to 4.75%, a 12.17 percentage-point improvement.

Approximately 35.4% of that improvement came from gross-margin expansion and 64.6% came from operating-expense leverage.

Gross-margin improvement was driven primarily by a more favorable revenue mix, including greater contribution from higher-margin Subscription revenue and reduced exposure to deeply negative-margin Hardware & Services. Operating-expense leverage was driven largely by improved G&A and R&D efficiency relative to revenue.

Management should prioritize three actions over the next 12–24 months.

## 1. Preserve Operating Leverage

Toast should continue growing operating expenses more slowly than revenue and gross profit through functional productivity, process improvement, automation, resource prioritization, and disciplined investment.

A modeled 1.0 percentage-point improvement in operating-expense efficiency would increase annual operating income by approximately $61.5M at the 2025 revenue base.

This should not be pursued through indiscriminate cost reduction. Management should protect investments required for continued growth, product competitiveness, and organizational capability.

## 2. Increase Subscription Product Adoption per Location

Toast should increase high-margin recurring revenue per location through broader software adoption, packaging, and selective monetization.

A modeled 5% increase in subscription revenue per location would generate approximately $46.8M in incremental annual subscription revenue and $33.6M in incremental gross profit at the 2025 baseline.

This initiative supports both growth and margin expansion, but management should validate attainable adoption uplift and customer response before committing significant resources.

## 3. Improve Hardware & Services Unit Economics Selectively

Toast should target clearly remediable inefficiencies in hardware procurement, fulfillment, installation, configuration, and professional-services delivery.

A modeled 20% reduction in the 2025 Hardware & Services gross loss would improve annual gross profit by approximately $44M.

However, management should not optimize this segment in isolation. Any cost-reduction program should first determine how Hardware & Services supports customer acquisition, activation, retention, and downstream Subscription and Fintech economics.

## Recommended Management Sequence

1. Preserve and extend operating leverage.
2. Increase Subscription product adoption and recurring revenue per location.
3. Selectively improve Hardware & Services economics after validating downstream customer-value effects.

## Decision Summary

The strongest path to further margin expansion is not a single cost-cutting initiative. Toast should combine continued operating discipline with growth in high-margin recurring revenue and targeted improvement of structurally weak unit economics.

The recommended portfolio of initiatives balances financial impact, growth preservation, evidence strength, and execution risk.

Scenario outputs are analytical sensitivities rather than company forecasts or guidance.
