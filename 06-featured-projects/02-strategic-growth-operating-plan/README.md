# Strategic Growth & Operating Plan

**Type:** Flagship

**Status:** Not Started

## Purpose

Demonstrate strategy, resource-allocation judgment, financial reasoning, prioritization, and execution planning in a management context.

The project evaluates how leadership should allocate limited resources across competing growth and operating initiatives.

## Core Question

> Given a defined growth or performance objective and limited resources, which initiatives should management prioritize over the next 12–24 months to achieve the target?

## Capabilities Demonstrated

- business-performance analysis
- strategic problem framing
- market and customer analysis
- resource allocation
- financial and ROI analysis
- scenario and sensitivity analysis
- initiative prioritization
- execution sequencing
- risk and dependency management
- KPI design
- executive recommendation

## Analytical Flow

Current Performance
→ Growth / Performance Gap
→ Strategic Alternatives
→ Market + Customer Evidence
→ Economics / ROI
→ Execution Requirements
→ Risks / Dependencies
→ Prioritization
→ 12–24 Month Operating Plan

## Potential Strategic Alternatives

- expand an existing product or service
- enter a new customer segment
- improve pricing or monetization
- improve retention
- automate a high-cost workflow
- increase sales capacity
- improve operational efficiency
- pursue a partnership
- selectively acquire a capability

## Decision Principle

Capital is only one constrained resource. Management must also allocate headcount, technical capacity, operating capacity, time, executive attention, and organizational change capacity.

## Planned Deliverables

1. Current-state business diagnostic
2. Strategic-alternatives assessment
3. Economic / ROI model
4. Prioritization framework
5. 12–24 month operating plan
6. Executive recommendation

## Portfolio Role

This project demonstrates the ability to move from business diagnosis to strategic prioritization and execution planning.
