# 12–24 Month Strategic Operating Plan

## Strategic Recommendation

CAVA should maintain disciplined restaurant expansion while shifting incremental management attention and investment toward same-store productivity and the infrastructure required to support a larger restaurant system.

The objective is not to reduce growth.

The objective is to improve the quality, capital efficiency, and operational sustainability of growth.

---

## Priority 1 — Improve Same-Store Productivity

### Objective

Increase economic output from the existing restaurant base while protecting customer experience and restaurant-level margins.

### Primary Initiatives

- Improve restaurant throughput during peak periods
- Increase labor productivity without degrading service quality
- Use loyalty and customer data to improve visit frequency
- Improve digital ordering and restaurant workflow integration
- Optimize menu mix and operational complexity
- Identify underperforming restaurants and replicate practices from stronger locations

### Base-Case Economic Opportunity

A 3% productivity uplift across approximately 500 restaurants produces:

- Approximately $46.5M of incremental annual revenue
- Approximately $10.9M of incremental restaurant-level profit

These figures are sensitivity estimates, not management guidance.

### Key Metrics

- Same-restaurant sales growth
- Traffic growth
- Average unit volume
- Restaurant-level margin
- Transactions per labor hour
- Customer frequency
- Digital mix
- Order throughput

---

## Priority 2 — Maintain Disciplined Restaurant Expansion

### Objective

Continue expanding the restaurant footprint while maintaining site quality, unit economics, and organizational capacity.

### Primary Initiatives

- Maintain rigorous site-selection standards
- Monitor new-unit ramp performance
- Track capital requirements by opening
- Protect restaurant-level economics during geographic expansion
- Match opening pace to management and training capacity
- Monitor construction and development bottlenecks

### Base-Case Economic Opportunity

Twenty incremental restaurants produce approximately:

- $62.0M of mature annual revenue
- $14.5M of mature restaurant-level profit
- $49.3M of modeled initial investment
- Approximately 3.4 years of mature payback

The payback estimate is directional because total capex is being used as a proxy for new-unit investment.

### Key Metrics

- Net new restaurant openings
- Capex per opening
- Pre-opening cost per opening
- New-unit AUV
- New-unit ramp time
- Restaurant-level margin
- Opening schedule adherence
- Payback period

---

## Priority 3 — Build Infrastructure for Scalable Growth

### Objective

Strengthen the systems, processes, people, and capabilities required to operate a significantly larger restaurant network.

### Primary Initiatives

- Expand field-management capacity
- Improve restaurant operating systems
- Strengthen training infrastructure
- Improve supply-chain scalability
- Build stronger restaurant performance analytics
- Standardize opening and operating processes
- Automate repetitive corporate and restaurant-support workflows where economically justified

### Base-Case Economic Opportunity

A 0.50 percentage-point improvement in corporate operating efficiency produces approximately:

- Approximately $5.8M of illustrative annual operating-income impact under a 0.50 percentage-point efficiency scenario

This understates the full strategic value because infrastructure also enables expansion and restaurant productivity.

### Key Metrics

- G&A as a percentage of revenue
- Management span of control
- Restaurant operating consistency
- Training readiness
- Time to open
- Supply-chain performance
- Margin stability
- Corporate productivity

---

# Phase 1 — 0 to 6 Months

## Focus

Establish the operating baseline and identify the highest-return productivity opportunities.

## Actions

- Diagnose restaurant-level productivity variation
- Identify throughput and labor bottlenecks
- Review new-unit ramp performance
- Establish capital-efficiency benchmarks
- Assess field-management capacity
- Identify infrastructure constraints before further acceleration

## Management Outcome

Create a fact base for allocating incremental resources across productivity, expansion, and infrastructure.

---

# Phase 2 — 6 to 12 Months

## Focus

Scale proven productivity initiatives while maintaining disciplined restaurant growth.

## Actions

- Deploy validated restaurant productivity initiatives
- Continue planned restaurant openings
- Invest selectively in enabling technology and training
- Monitor unit economics by restaurant cohort
- Track capital efficiency and opening performance
- Adjust opening pace where operating capacity becomes constrained

## Management Outcome

Increase revenue and restaurant-level profit without allowing expansion velocity to weaken unit economics.

---

# Phase 3 — 12 to 24 Months

## Focus

Scale the operating model while preserving growth quality.

## Actions

- Expand successful productivity programs systemwide
- Increase restaurant development where economics remain attractive
- Strengthen supply chain and field leadership for the larger footprint
- Automate and standardize scalable operating processes
- Reallocate capital based on observed ROI and payback
- Reassess expansion pace against restaurant and corporate capacity

## Management Outcome

Create a repeatable growth model capable of supporting a substantially larger restaurant base without sacrificing economic performance.

---

# Executive Decision Rule

CAVA should accelerate investment only when growth passes three tests:

1. Unit economics remain attractive
2. Organizational capacity can support the additional scale
3. Incremental capital produces better risk-adjusted returns than competing uses

This framework prevents growth from becoming an objective independent of economic value.
