# Strategic Growth & Operating Plan — Data Requirements

## Objective

Collect the minimum public data required to evaluate CAVA's current growth model, unit economics, expansion capacity, and resource-allocation trade-offs over the next 12–24 months.

---

## 1. Growth & Scale

Required metrics:

- total revenue
- year-over-year revenue growth
- restaurant count
- net new restaurant openings
- opening growth rate
- geographic expansion
- average unit volume (AUV)
- same-restaurant sales growth
- traffic growth
- average check / pricing contribution, if disclosed

Purpose:

Determine whether growth is being driven primarily by:
- new unit expansion
- existing-store productivity
- pricing
- traffic
- mix

---

## 2. Restaurant-Level Economics

Required metrics:

- restaurant-level revenue
- restaurant-level profit
- restaurant-level profit margin
- food / beverage / packaging costs
- labor costs
- occupancy costs
- other restaurant operating costs
- pre-opening costs

Purpose:

Evaluate whether rapid expansion is preserving or diluting restaurant economics.

---

## 3. Corporate Cost Structure

Required metrics:

- general and administrative expense
- G&A as a percentage of revenue
- operating income
- operating margin
- stock-based compensation, if material
- depreciation and amortization, if useful

Purpose:

Determine whether corporate infrastructure is scaling efficiently as the restaurant base grows.

---

## 4. Capital Allocation

Required metrics:

- capital expenditures
- cash and equivalents
- operating cash flow
- free cash flow, if calculable
- restaurant opening / build costs, if disclosed
- lease obligations, if relevant

Purpose:

Estimate how much financial capacity CAVA has to fund growth and whether expansion is becoming more capital intensive.

---

## 5. Expansion Capacity

Required evidence:

- annual restaurant opening targets
- long-term restaurant-count targets
- real-estate strategy
- construction / development commentary
- hiring and training requirements
- field-management capacity
- supply-chain investments
- manufacturing / commissary capacity, if applicable

Purpose:

Identify non-financial bottlenecks that could constrain expansion.

---

## 6. Productivity & Customer Economics

Required metrics or evidence:

- digital sales mix
- loyalty membership / engagement
- throughput initiatives
- restaurant labor productivity
- order volumes
- menu innovation
- catering / new channels
- daypart expansion
- customer frequency, if disclosed

Purpose:

Evaluate whether CAVA can drive more economic value from the existing restaurant base.

---

## 7. Strategic Infrastructure

Required evidence:

- technology investments
- restaurant systems
- supply-chain investments
- automation
- analytics
- training systems
- operational standardization
- management hiring
- support-center investments

Purpose:

Assess whether infrastructure investment could improve scalability, restaurant productivity, or execution quality.

---

## 8. Management Guidance & Strategic Priorities

Required evidence:

- 2026 guidance
- 2027 targets, if available
- long-term growth targets
- expansion priorities
- margin expectations
- management commentary on growth constraints
- management commentary on investment priorities

Purpose:

Separate management's stated strategy from our independent recommendation.

---

## 9. Modeling Inputs

Potential scenario inputs:

### Expansion
- number of incremental restaurant openings
- revenue per new restaurant
- restaurant-level margin
- opening cost
- ramp period

### Same-Store Productivity
- traffic improvement
- AUV improvement
- margin improvement
- digital / loyalty uplift
- implementation cost

### Infrastructure
- incremental G&A / technology investment
- productivity benefit
- restaurant-margin impact
- expansion-capacity impact
- time to value

All non-reported inputs must be explicitly labeled as assumptions.

---

## 10. Source Priority

Use sources in this order:

1. CAVA 10-K
2. CAVA quarterly earnings releases / 10-Qs
3. CAVA investor presentations
4. earnings-call transcripts
5. other high-quality public sources only when company disclosures are insufficient

---

## Minimum Dataset

The initial structured dataset should contain at least:

- year / quarter
- revenue
- restaurant count
- net openings
- same-restaurant sales growth
- traffic growth
- AUV
- restaurant-level profit
- restaurant-level margin
- G&A
- operating income
- operating margin
- pre-opening costs
- capital expenditures

The dataset should cover at least 2023–2026 where comparable public data is available.

---

## Data Quality Rules

- Never mix fiscal periods without labeling them.
- Do not infer missing values unless explicitly calculated from reported data.
- Preserve source references for reported metrics.
- Separate reported data from calculated metrics.
- Flag any inconsistent definitions across reporting periods.
- Do not treat management guidance as realized performance.
